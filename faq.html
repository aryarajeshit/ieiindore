Frequently Asked Questions

How can I apply for IEI membership?

You can apply online through the official IEI portal by creating an account and submitting required documents.

👉 Apply for Membership

What are the membership categories?

IEI offers Student Member (SMIE), Associate Member (AMIE), Member (MIE), and Fellow (FIE) based on qualification and experience.

👉 View Membership Details

How can I register for IEI events?

Event registrations are available through the IEI website or announcements from local centres and student chapters.

👉 View Events

How can I submit a research paper?

Papers can be submitted through IEI–Springer journal portals following the prescribed guidelines and peer-review process.

👉 Submit Paper

How to start an IEI Student Chapter?

Institutions need to apply through IEI with a faculty coordinator, student members, and basic infrastructure.

How can organizations collaborate with IEI?

Organizations can sponsor or partner in events, research initiatives, and technical programmes by contacting IEI centres.
